🎯 What Makes Us Different
Radical transparency in every order
🎥
Live Kitchen Streaming — Per Order
Every order triggers a live RTSP camera stream of your food being prepared. Watch our chef cook your Paneer Butter Masala or Litti Chokha in real-time. India's first cloud kitchen with per-order live video.
🧼
100% Transparent Hygiene
No hidden corners. Our open kitchen design ensures you see the full preparation area — clean countertops, gloved hands, covered ingredients. If it's not clean enough to stream, it's not clean enough to cook in.
🏠
Authentic Home-Style Recipes
Our menu celebrates India's regional food heritage — Indori Poha, Bihari Litti Chokha, Sattu Paratha, Rajma Chawal. Recipes perfected over generations, not engineered for industrial production.

Real-Time Rider GPS Tracking
Know exactly where your delivery is — live on the map. Track your rider from our kitchen at Hebbal to your doorstep with 5-second location updates. No more "out for delivery" black boxes.

30-Minute Average Delivery
Single kitchen, focused menu, efficient riders — we've optimised our end-to-end process for speed without compromising freshness. 8–10 km radius from Hebbal means shorter distances, faster delivery.
